Neo Bloxorz Privacy Policy

Ghorbel Games · Last updated: October 7, 2026

Neo Bloxorz ("the game") is a puzzle game made by Ghorbel Games. This policy explains what information the game and its advertising partner handle when you play.

What the game stores

Your progress (levels solved, best scores, streaks, a run in progress) and your settings are saved only on your device. The game has no accounts, and we do not collect, send or sell this information. Uninstalling the game, or using Settings › Reset all progress, deletes it.

Advertising

The Android app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language);
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostics.

Google handles this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings.

Rewarded ads (for example to continue a run) are always optional.

Web version

The browser version of the game shows no ads and stores your progress in your browser's local storage only.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/Ghorbel37/neo-bloxorz/issues.
