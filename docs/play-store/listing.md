```
Every move changes your block's shape.

Swipe sideways and it rolls over and changes width. Swipe up or down and it changes height. Small square, long bar, big square: rolling keeps a steady rhythm, so the goal right next to you can be just out of reach. Bump into a wall to change shape in place, shift your rhythm, and land in the glowing goal in exactly the right shape.

Easy to learn, surprisingly deep: a coached tutorial introduces each idea right when you need it.

• 36 handcrafted puzzles in 4 worlds: Bump, Glass, Shapes and Circuits
• Glass that cracks under the big square, colored tiles that hold only one shape, switches that flip bridges
• Descent: a roguelike run through endless puzzle floors with hearts, move budgets and perks
• Rush: beat the clock, every solve buys more time
• Daily: a fresh puzzle every day, keep your streak alive
• Precision: replay the campaign with a strict move limit
• Hints, undo, and dashed outlines that show where each swipe will land
• Every generated puzzle is checked by a solver: always solvable
• Plays offline, no account needed

Made by Ghorbel Games.
```
