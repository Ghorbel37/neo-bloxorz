## Neo Bloxorz 1.3.2

- **Stronger vibration.** Wall bumps and switches used a "light impact" (half strength, 50 ms)
  that many phones can't make you feel. Every buzz is now a plain vibration at your phone's
  normal strength: short for bumps and switches, longer for falls, losing a heart and time running out.
- **Settings › Test vibration** buzzes for 0.4 s and shows what happened on your phone (which
  vibration system the app found, and any error). If you feel nothing and there is no error,
  check that the phone isn't in silent or Do Not Disturb mode and that vibration/haptic feedback
  is turned on in the phone's settings.

### Install
Download `neo-bloxorz-v1.3.2-debug.apk` below and open it on your Android phone. It installs
over earlier versions and keeps your progress.
