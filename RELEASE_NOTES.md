## Neo Bloxorz 1.0.0

A 2D shape-shifting block puzzle. Every move rolls the block over its edge:
← → toggles its width, ↑ ↓ toggles its height. Land exactly in the goal.

### Install
Download `neo-bloxorz-v1.0.0.apk` below and open it on your Android phone
(allow "install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager when asked).

### What's inside
- **Campaign**: 30 puzzles in 3 worlds (Basics, Glass, Circuits) with stars, best scores and hints.
- **Descent** (roguelike): endless generated floors, 3 hearts, a move budget per floor and a perk after every floor
  (Extra Heart, Rewind, Stamina, Ghost, Compass, Warp). Runs are saved when you leave.
- **Rush** (time attack): 90 seconds, every solve buys time, puzzles get harder.
- **Daily**: one new puzzle a day, with a streak counter.
- **Precision**: campaign levels with a strict move limit. No undo, no hints.
- Swipe, on-screen arrows or keyboard controls; undo; synthesized sound effects; vibration; settings.
