## Neo Bloxorz 1.5.0: a separate tutorial

- **Tutorial, separate from the levels.** It plays the first time you open the game: three short
  guided stages (rolling, bumping walls, the rhythm). Skip it any time, and replay it from
  **Settings › Replay tutorial**.
- **Levels never restrict your moves any more.** When a level introduces something new (walls,
  glass, shape tiles, switches, heavy switches), a note explains it and highlights the tiles, but
  every swipe works. After your first move the note moves to the hint line under the board.
- The campaign is now 34 levels: the two teaching-only boards moved into the tutorial.

### Install
Download `neo-bloxorz-v1.5.0-debug.apk` below and open it. It installs over your current version
and keeps your progress.
