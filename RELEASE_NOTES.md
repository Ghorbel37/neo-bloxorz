## Neo Bloxorz 1.1.0: real puzzles

Version 1.0 was a maze: since every move changes the block's shape, its shape was decided by
where it stood, and every level came down to following the one path.

1.1 adds **walls**. Rolling into a wall makes the block change shape in place, which shifts its
rhythm. The goal is often right next to you but out of step, and the puzzle is figuring out which
walls to bump, and in what order, to land in it with the right shape.

- New 32-level campaign in 4 worlds: **Bump**, **Glass**, **Shapes** (tiles that only hold one
  shape) and **Circuits** (switches and bridges). Every level after the first tutorial is
  verified to need wall bumps.
- Descent, Rush and Daily now generate puzzles of the same kind, getting harder as you go.
- Dashed outlines show where each swipe would land (toggle in Settings).
- New sounds for bumping a wall and for a blocked move.

### Install
Download `neo-bloxorz-v1.1.0.apk` below and open it on your Android phone (allow installing
apps from your browser or file manager when asked). It installs over 1.0.0.
