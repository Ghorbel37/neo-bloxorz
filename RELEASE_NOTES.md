## Neo Bloxorz 1.3.0: Ghorbel Games, ads, and getting ready for Google Play

- **Ghorbel Games** brand splash at launch (tap to skip), and credits in Settings.
- **Ads** (Google AdMob, currently Google's test ads):
  - a banner on the menus only, never over the puzzle;
  - a full-screen ad after losing, at most every second loss and never twice in 90 seconds;
  - optional rewarded ads: continue a Descent run with one heart (once per run), +30 seconds
    in Rush (once per session), or a hint when you're out of hints in Descent.
  - consent prompt where the law requires it, and "Privacy choices" in Settings.
  - "Remove ads" in Settings (the purchase itself comes once the game is on Google Play).
- Fixed: on small phones the last mode on the home screen was hidden behind the buttons.
- Google Play preparation: signed AAB builds in CI (once the upload key is added), privacy
  policy, store listing text, screenshots and feature graphic, and a step-by-step guide.

### Install
Download `neo-bloxorz-v1.3.0-debug.apk` below and open it on your Android phone. It installs
over earlier versions and keeps your progress.
