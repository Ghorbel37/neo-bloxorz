## Neo Bloxorz 1.6.1

- **Glass is back**, now drawn as **cracked floor** with a crossed-out big square on it, so
  it's clear the big square will break through and fall. (1.6.0's gates are gone.) World 2 is
  now called **Cracks**; your stars carry over.
- **Holes always lose.** Rolling onto a hole now always drops the block, even when a wall
  stands next to it (before, the wall won and you bumped). A bump also never stretches the
  block out over a hole or off the board: the wall flashes and the block stays put. Levels
  that relied on the old behavior got walls in place of those holes.
- **New first level.** The Sketch now has holes, so it needs a different route from the tutorial.
- **Harder Precision.** Solve in exactly par moves, with no undo, hints or landing previews.
- **Modes unlock with stars**: Daily at ★ 6, Rush at ★ 15, Descent at ★ 24, Precision at ★ 36.
  Modes you've already played stay open.

### Install
Download `neo-bloxorz-v1.6.1-debug.apk` below and open it. It installs over your current version
and keeps your progress.
