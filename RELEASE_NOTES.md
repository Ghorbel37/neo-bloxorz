## Neo Bloxorz 1.3.1

- **Fixed: vibration now works in the Android app.** It used the browser vibration API, which
  does nothing in the app without the vibration permission. It now uses native haptics:
  light ticks for wall bumps and switches, a stronger buzz for falls and losing hearts.
  It can still be turned off in Settings.

### Install
Download `neo-bloxorz-v1.3.1-debug.apk` below and open it on your Android phone. It installs
over earlier versions and keeps your progress.
